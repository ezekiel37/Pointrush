# Acticlaim vision

Where Acticlaim is going after launch, and the rules we build by. Plain language on purpose. Dated October 2026; update it when decisions change.

## In one sentence

Acticlaim gives Nigerian small businesses, creators and solopreneurs an AI team that is paid for verified results.

## Why

- Most Nigerian small businesses try AI and give up: tools need setting up, skills they do not have, and trust they will not give them with money or customers. Reported AI adoption among Nigerian businesses is about 10% (Q1 2026).
- Reach is their biggest marketing problem: studies citing SMEDAN report about 62% say their marketing reach is limited and 58% struggle to win customers on a small budget.
- Only about 4% of Nigerian small businesses can access credit, and they receive about 1% of bank loans.
- More people are becoming entrepreneurs and creators, but most earn little: 6 in 10 African creators earn under $100 a month, and brand sponsorships, not ad revenue, are their main income.

Figures are from secondary sources (see Sources); treat them as indicative.

## What we already have

Verified outcome rails, live in sandbox:

- Businesses prepay rewards before an offer goes live.
- Customers and taskers are paid only for a purchase confirmed at the till, a prize code claimed (every code wins) or a task approved.
- Wallet, withdrawals through Bachs, reviewer tools, void disputes, per-business staff, help centre.

Outcome-based AI needs exactly this: money locked in advance, proof the outcome happened, payment only when it did, and people available to check. That is our edge.

## Who it is for

- **Businesses**: shops, restaurants, salons, pharmacies, brands, startups, solopreneurs and creators who run a business.
- **People**: customers (cash back, prize codes) and taskers (paid tasks, human checks, promoting businesses for verified outcomes). No other user types.

## Roadmap

One thing at a time. Each phase starts only when the one before it is used by real businesses.

### Phase 1: agent toolkit in the business dashboard (next 6 months)

1. **Campaign agent** (first): plans, writes and adjusts cash back, prize code and task campaigns, and suggests reward size and budget. The owner approves before anything goes live or spends money.
2. **Books agent**: keeps simple accounts from verified Acticlaim sales and payouts, drafts payment reminders by email or SMS, and sends a weekly "what came in, what went out" summary.
3. **Support agent**: a web chat widget or link for the business's website, Linktree or bio. Answers from the business's own information and hands off to the owner for refunds, complaints and money.
4. **Taskers as human checkers**: when an agent is unsure (is this delivery photo valid, is this review genuine), it creates a small paid task for a verified tasker.

### Phase 2: social agents, one platform at a time

Acticlaim registers one developer app per platform. Each business connects its own account to our app with one click; agents act for that business; we charge for the service and pass platform costs on openly. We never share or resell API keys or tokens (platforms ban that); we sell our service, which each business authorizes.

### Phase 3: analytics

Dashboards from verified data: new versus returning customers, campaign return, cash flow, seasonality, with a plain-language weekly brief from the books agent.

### Phase 4: credit information

Turn verified sales and cash flow into a credit profile the business owns and can share, with its consent, with lenders (cash-flow lenders and banks). Acticlaim does not lend and does not run a credit bureau; both are licensed activities. We are the trusted data source.

### Creators: paid for outcomes, not views

Creators and taskers promote businesses and are paid per verified new customer or purchase, not per post. Businesses stop paying for empty reach; small creators earn from real results.

## Principles

1. **One business, one private space.** Agents, activity logs, customers, conversations and data belong to one business. No other business, and no other business's agents, can see or query them. Enforced in the database, as wallets and campaigns are today.
2. **Everything an agent does is logged** and visible to that business: what, when, why, and who approved it.
3. **Owners approve anything public or involving money.** Agents draft; people decide. No agent moves money on its own.
4. **People step in at risky moments only**, with routine items batched into one daily review, so approval does not become a reflex.
5. **Each business connects its own accounts.** No shared credentials.
6. **Costs are passed on openly**, never hidden inside a flat fee.
7. **Credit data is shared only with consent.** Acticlaim never lends.
8. **Funds stay with the payment provider.** Campaign money is held through Bachs; we do not take deposits.

## What we are not doing

- No ajo, esusu or pooled savings.
- No WhatsApp by default; only when a business covers the cost.
- No lending and no credit bureau.
- No games of chance: every prize code wins.
- No users under 18.

## Business model

- Rewards and task fees on verified outcomes (today).
- Agents: charged per outcome where we can verify it (a resolved conversation, a confirmed sale, a new customer), from the business's prepaid balance.
- Social agents: platform costs passed on with a clear margin.
- Later: analytics subscriptions and consent-based data sharing with lenders.

## Risks to manage

- **Scope.** Build the campaign agent first and prove businesses pay for it before the next.
- **Agent mistakes.** Wrong promises or prices create liability: limits, owner approval, logs and hand-off.
- **Platform bans.** One shared app means one abusive business can get the whole app suspended: per-business limits and abuse monitoring from day one.
- **Privacy.** Books and credit data are sensitive under the Nigeria Data Protection Act: clear consent, minimal data, sharing only on request.
- **Language quality.** Test Nigerian English and Pidgin with real traders before promising other languages.
- **Distribution.** The hardest problem is getting businesses, not technology. Show each agent to 10 real owners before building the next.
